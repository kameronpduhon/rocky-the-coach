## Goal

- **Primary:** recomp. Lose fat (midsection, face) while building muscle, with arms and shoulders as the priority.
- **Looks like:** arms fill out t-shirts, leaner midsection and face, visible veins and muscle definition over time.
- **Checkpoint:** Jan 1, 2027 (about 13 weeks). Target is the visible start of changes, not the finish line.
- **Pace:** moderate. No crash dieting.
- **Starting point:** returning lifter, back in the gym about 3 to 4 weeks as of 2026-10-03. Muscle memory works in our favor.
- **Jan 1 checkpoint:** about 196 lb (195 to 198 is a win), size 34 pants fit comfortably, waist down 1 to 1.5 in from the first measurement.
- **Long-term target:** roughly 185 to 190 lb at around 12% body fat. That is where veins and abs start showing. Expect spring or summer 2027, not Jan 1.
- Scale moves slower during a recomp because muscle is going on while fat comes off. Waist and photos are the truer signal.

## Training

- **Frequency:** 3 required days, weekdays, between 12 and 2pm. About 45 minutes each.
- **Style:** heavy, 2 sets per exercise, taken to failure.
- **Failure safety:** true failure on machines and cables. One rep short on free-weight presses without a spotter.
- **Rest:** 2 to 3 minutes on presses and rows, about 90 seconds on isolation work.
- **Warm-up:** 1 to 2 lighter sets before the first exercise for each muscle. Not to failure, not counted.
- **Likes:** cables, pec deck, dumbbells, preacher curl. **Avoid:** squats, lunges.
- **Priority:** arms twice a week. Chest and shoulders twice a week each.
- **Legs:** trained normally. Leg exercises do not bother the feet.

### Cardio

- **Ruled out:** running, jogging, anything hard on the feet. Biking (tried it, hated it).
- **Main cardio:** regular walking, tracked as a daily step goal.
- **Occasional:** StairMaster now and then, optional.
- **Daily steps:** start at 7,000. Add 500 a week if it felt fine, cap at 10,000.
- **Feet rule:** break walking into chunks under 30 minutes (feet start hurting around 45 minutes straight). Post-lunch walk, evening walk, standing desk time.

## Nutrition

- **Calories:** 2,400 a day. Estimated maintenance is about 2,800 to 2,900, so this is a ~450 calorie deficit, around 1 lb a week. It is a starting estimate: after 2 to 3 weeks the real weigh-in trend sets the true number.
- **Protein:** 180 g a day minimum, about 60 g per meal.
- **Fat and carbs:** flexible. Hit calories and protein; the rest sorts itself out.
- **Tracking:** hybrid. Meals come from a personal library with the numbers pre-calculated. The app gives the gram amount of each ingredient to put on the food scale. No math on Kameron's end, ever (raw vs cooked included). Only off-plan food gets logged by hand.
- **Proteins:** chicken, shrimp, fish, beef. Ground beef about once a week max.
- **Carbs:** potatoes, rice. Not picky; likes most foods.
- **Weekdays:** animal-based. Meat first, plus fruit, honey, dairy, potatoes, rice.
- **Weekends:** meat-first whole foods. Protein at every meal, anything unprocessed.
- **Always:** cut processed food as much as possible.
- **Meals:** 3 a day (breakfast, lunch, dinner).
- **Afternoon snack (3 to 4pm):** planned and high-protein instead of random. Examples: Greek yogurt, jerky, cottage cheese, fruit.
- **Dessert (8pm):** replaced, not banned. Animal-based options like Greek yogurt with honey and berries, or fruit with whipped cream. Counted inside the 2,400.
- **Breakfast:** cereal is out. Eggs, meat, Greek yogurt, fruit.
- **Variety rule:** no eating the same thing day after day or week after week. Repetition is the #1 quit trigger.
- **Prep:** no set prep days. Cook when food is needed, either 1 portion or a 2 to 3 day batch. Every week will look different.
- **Meal library:** starter library in `docs/meals.md`.
- **Eating out:** out to eat every weekend. 1 to 2 relaxed meals per weekend. No set orders: apply the meat-first whole foods rule at whatever restaurant it is.
- **Alcohol:** rare, less than once a week. 1 to 2 Ranch Waters (~100 calories each), counted inside the day's calories.
- **Supplements:** none. Protein powder on hand as a backup for hitting 180 g protein.
- **Water:** already drinking plenty. Not tracked.

## Recovery

- Rest days: weekends, plus any weekday without a session

## Old ways and if-then rules

### Triggers

- **Meal burnout:** eating the same thing until sick of it, then quitting. The #1 historical quit pattern.
- **Boredom snacking:** nothing to do, so grab a snack.
- **8pm dessert:** cookies, ice cream. The biggest leak in the current diet.
- **3 to 4pm snack** going unhealthy.
- **Sugary cereal** at breakfast.
- **Skipping workouts** every now and then.
- **Sitting all day.** Standing desk goes unused.

### Warning signs (what slips first)

1. A skipped workout.
2. Getting tired of the meals.

The app watches for both: a missed training day gets a same-day nudge toward Plan B, and meal rotation keeps repeats down before burnout sets in.

### If-then rules

| Trigger | Rule |
|---|---|
| 8pm, want cookies or ice cream | Have the planned dessert. Kitchen closes after it. |
| 3 to 4pm snack craving | Eat the planned protein snack. Nothing else until dinner. |
| Bored and reaching for a snack | Water and a 10-minute walk first. Still hungry after? Eat something from the plan. |
| Skipping the gym on a training day | Do the Plan B home workout that evening. No zero days. |
| Weekend restaurant | Order protein first, then enjoy it. One relaxed meal is not a ruined weekend. |
| Sitting all afternoon | Stand at the desk after lunch, then a 15-minute walk. |

### Minimum viable day

What still counts as "on plan" on the worst day:

- Hit 180 g protein
- Hit the step goal
- No off-plan dessert

### Miss rule

Never miss twice. One bad meal or missed session is normal. Two in a row is the pattern Rocky calls out.

### Coach tone

Straight shooter with encouragement. Direct about what slipped, then focused on the next right move. Never preachy, never piles on.

## Tracking and check-ins

### Daily log (under 1 minute, logged in the moment)

- Morning weigh-in
- Meals checked off as they are eaten
- Sets logged during the workout
- Steps pulled in automatically from the Apple Watch

Logging happens live, not from memory the next day. If a meal is not checked off near its usual time, the app nudges.

### Weekly check-in (Sunday evening, about 10 minutes)

- 7-day average weight vs last week, waist, days on plan
- Pick next week's meals, app builds the grocery list
- Flag meals that are getting old so the app rests them
- Progress photos every 4 weeks

### Adjustment rules

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| Losing less than 0.5 lb a week for 2 weeks, on plan | Cut 150 calories or add 1,000 daily steps |
| Losing more than 2 lb a week for 2 weeks | Add 150 calories to protect muscle |
| Weight stuck, not on plan | No number changes. Fix adherence first |
