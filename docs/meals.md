# Starter Meal Library

The seed library the app starts with. It grows over time; meals flagged as "getting old" at the Sunday check-in get rested.

**How the numbers work:** amounts are what goes on the food scale for **1 portion**. Meat, fish, and potatoes are weighed raw. Rice is weighed cooked. Calories and protein are rounded estimates; the app recalculates exact numbers from USDA FoodData Central and scales everything for batch cooking.

**Daily budget:** 2,400 calories, 180 g protein. A typical day:

| Slot | Calories | Protein |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | ~600 | ~55 g |
| Lunch | ~650 | ~55 g |
| Dinner | ~650 | ~55 g |
| Afternoon snack | ~200 to 250 | ~25 g |
| Dessert | ~200 | varies |
