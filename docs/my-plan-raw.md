# My Plan (raw, Step 1)

Written 2026-10-03. Kept as-is; the cleaned-up version becomes `docs/plan.md` in Step 3.

## Goal

Look like I work out: arms fill out t-shirts. Lose fat in midsection and face. Visible veins/muscles if possible. Not in a rush for results, maybe by the new year (Jan 1, 2027) to see the start of changes.

## Training

Go to my local gym a minimum of three times a week, doing a ~45 minute workout. Hit at least 2 body parts a workout. 2 sets per workout, heavy weight till failure.

## Nutrition

Some form of carnivore diet. Cut out as much processed food as possible. Make sure I get enough protein to grow muscles and stay healthy.

## Rules already decided

- No running or hard-on-feet cardio. I have feet problems. I would love to run and jog but it hurts too much.
- Won't be going to the gym 7 days a week for over an hour.
- I do not want to eat the same thing every day, so no meal prep for a whole week (maybe 2 to 3 days I can do).
- I do not want to eat the same thing week after week. It normally starts out good, but I get burnt out and quit.

## Schedule

- Work 5 days a week.
- Train weekdays around 12 to 2.
- Eat ~3 times a day: breakfast, lunch, dinner.
